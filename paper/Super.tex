\begin{center}\textbf{Summary}\end{center}
 
\vspace{-0.5\baselineskip}
 
The thesis covers Six European Union member states that kept their own currencies for the whole of the study period from 2005 to 2024. 
Using GARCH model estimates the daily conditional volatility of each currency against the euro.
The series enter a panel growth regression together with macroeconomic variables, including euro area trade intensity and investment, inflation and government expenditure.
